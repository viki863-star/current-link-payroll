About Us

Moving Mountains,
Delivering Excellence

Current Link Transport & General Contracting LLC SPC, based in Mussaffah, Abu Dhabi, has been a trusted provider of heavy equipment, water tankers, and transport solutions since 2015. We serve leading construction, infrastructure, and industrial companies across the UAE with well-maintained equipment and experienced, trained operators on monthly rental basis.

From water supply solutions to heavy lifting and specialized transport, we deliver reliability, safety, and 24/7 operational support — ensuring our clients' projects stay on track.
