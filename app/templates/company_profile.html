About Us

Moving Mountains,
Delivering Excellence

Current Link Transport & General Contracting LLC SPC, based in Mussaffah, Abu Dhabi, has been a trusted provider of heavy equipment, water tankers, and transport solutions since 2015. We serve leading construction, infrastructure, and industrial companies across the UAE with a modern, well-maintained fleet.

Our clients rely on us for monthly rental of heavy equipment with trained, experienced operators who ensure safe and efficient project execution. We are a licensed SPC company under Abu Dhabi's regulatory framework.

Why Choose Us

Quality, Safety & Reliability

Our commitment to safety, quality, and client satisfaction sets us apart. Every operation follows strict safety protocols and maintenance standards.

Safety First

All equipment undergoes regular safety inspections. Operators are trained in HSE protocols and safe operating procedures. We maintain a zero-incident culture on every project site.

Experienced Team

Our operators average 8+ years of UAE experience. Each team member is vetted, trained, and committed to delivering professional service on every assignment.

Well-Maintained Fleet

Every unit is serviced on a strict schedule. We maintain detailed maintenance records and carry out pre-rental inspections to ensure equipment is always in top condition.

24/7 Availability

Our operations team is available around the clock. Emergency breakdown support, after-hours deliveries, and weekend service are part of our standard offering.

ICV & ISO Certified

We hold ICV certification, ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), and are VMS certified as a registered vendor with major UAE contractors and government entities.

UAE-Wide Coverage

We serve clients across all seven emirates — from Abu Dhabi to Dubai, Sharjah, Al Ain, and the Northern Emirates. Our fleet is strategically positioned for quick deployment.
