Who We Are

About Current Link

Current Link Transport & General Contracting LLC SPC is based in Confident property management building, Behind Aramex, First Floor, B-Block -office 102, M9, Mussaffah — serving the UAE with comprehensive heavy equipment and transport solutions since 2015.

We specialize in water supply, water removal, and heavy equipment hire including excavators, cranes, man lifts, trailers, forklifts, bobcats, and graders. All equipment is provided with experienced, trained operators on monthly rental basis to leading companies across the UAE — delivering reliable, on-time service backed by 24/7 support and a well-maintained fleet.
