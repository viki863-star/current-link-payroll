Who We Are

About Current Link

Current Link Transport & General Contracting LLC SPC is based in Mussafah M17, Abu Dhabi — serving the UAE with comprehensive heavy equipment and transport solutions since 2015.

We specialize in water supply, water removal, and heavy equipment hire including excavators, cranes, man lifts, trailers, forklifts, bobcats, and graders. Our equipment is provided with experienced, trained operators on monthly rental basis to leading companies including NMDC and other major contractors across the region.
